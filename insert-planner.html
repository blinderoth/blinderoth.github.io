                Export

                STL files contain visible printed parts only, with any resizing or trimming, and come zipped.

                "One STL" keeps every part where it sits in the box; "own STL" puts each part flat at the origin, ready for the slicer. The drawing has an assembled view on page 1 and an exploded view on page 2, each with numbered parts and a parts list.
