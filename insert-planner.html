          Printer and material

          Export

          STL files contain visible printed parts only, with any resizing or trimming, and come zipped.

          "One STL" keeps every part where it sits in the box; "own STL" puts each part flat at the origin, ready for the slicer. The drawing has an assembled view on page 1 and an exploded view on page 2, each with numbered parts and a parts list.

          Help
          Mouse
          Click a piece to select it, Shift/Ctrl-click for several. Drag the arrows to move it. Drag empty space to orbit, scroll to zoom. Alt-drag to drag a copy.

          Keys
          Arrows nudge · R rotate · D drop down
          M measure · F free space
          H hide · L lock · Del remove
          Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Y undo / redo · Ctrl+A select all

          Selected

          Click empty space to deselect

          Position

          Turn and place

          Tilt

          Tilts game components so a deck or board can lean in the box. Press Drop down afterwards to settle it.

          Scale

          Type a new size in mm to scale straight away, or set percentages and press Apply. Untick Keep proportions to change one axis only.

          Resize or trim

          Free space

          Largest empty boxes that fit, measured from the tops of pieces up to the lid. Clearance is already subtracted.
