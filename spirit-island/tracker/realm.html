The Island

Each adversary holds a stretch of the island, with levels 0 to 6 running from the coast to the heart. A win grows the land back, a loss leaves it blighted, and untried ground stays bare. Scenarios wait offshore as landmarks.

The Tides

Every invasion in the order it was fought. The wave rises with the score; foam is surviving Dahan, sludge is blight left on the island. Drag the handles under the tide to choose a stretch of time, and every chapter on the page follows.

The Weather

The elements your spirits bring to the island, averaged per spirit. One shape for the games you won, one for the games you lost. Pick a guardian to read their sky alone.

The Constellation

Each spirit is a star, sized by games and coloured by win rate. Lines join spirits that fought side by side. Hover a star to look, click it to keep it lit.

Anatomy of a Score

Every score taken apart. A win earns five points per difficulty, a ten-point bonus and two per invader card left; a loss earns two per difficulty and one per invader card out. Dahan minus blight, shared between players, tips the balance either way.

The Blight Jars

Every lost game, bottled. Each jar holds the blight left on the island when the invaders won, shelved by how the game was lost.

The Lineages

Each spirit's record, read left to right through time. A stripe marks a game it played: green for a win, red for a loss, taller for harder. Gaps are the games it sat out.

Your Next Invasion

The island deals a challenge from what you own and have not yet mastered: spirits without a win, pairings never tried, the next adversary level to beat and scenarios still unwon. It reads every game, whatever the time window says.
